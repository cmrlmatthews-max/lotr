About this map

A personal, non-commercial explorer for Tolkien's world. It has two sheets: the north-west of Middle-earth at the end of the Third Age, and Beleriand in the First Age. Use the buttons below the title to change the sheet.

How to move

  • Drag to move. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Shift-drag or right-drag to turn and tilt.
  • Click a name to open its card. Double-click a name or the land to fly there.
  • / search   Esc close   space play a journey   ← → journey stages

How to read the cards

  • Book text gives only what Tolkien's published books say.
  • Film text gives what the Peter Jackson films show or change. Series text gives the same for The Rings of Power.
  • An AI model (Claude) wrote the card text in its own words and a second pass reviewed it. Errors are possible. Each card shows a confidence level and where to read more.

Map data and credit

Terrain and map layers: Rose, Robert A. (2020), GIS & Middle Earth Presentation & Data Set, William & Mary, doi.org/10.21220/RKEZ-X707, built on the work of the ME-DEM project. License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0. The reduced terrain and the vector layers in this file are an adaptation of that data set and keep the same license.

Beleriand: no survey data exists for this land. The terrain is an original model made for this map from the descriptions in The Silmarillion, Unfinished Tales and The Children of Húrin. It follows the distances that the books give, but positions are approximate.

3D engine: three.js (MIT license). Middle-earth and its names are the creation of J.R.R. Tolkien. This file has no connection with the Tolkien Estate, the publishers, or the film and series makers.
